## Project: Pizza Sales Performance Analysis

**Project link:** https://github.com/NTMASKOSTALIN/Pizza-Sales-Performance

**Code:** https://github.com/NTMASKOSTALIN/Pizza-Sales-Performance/blob/main/pizza_sales-%20sql.sql

**Goal:** Revenue Growth & Menu Optimization

**Description:** The project focuses on the dataset of Pizza sales for the year 2015. The dataset contains 48k+ rows with 4 different tables Orders, Order details, Pizzas &  Pizza types, it contains relevant business information to achieve the project goal. The project involves loading the data, profiling, data validation, Joins verification, Master table creation, Tableau Dashboarding and Advanced analysis(Product performance matrix: Revenue Vs Volume & Pareto Analysis).

**Skills:** data profiling, data analysis, statistical analysis & data visualization.

**Technology:** SQL & Tableau

**Dashboard:** https://public.tableau.com/app/profile/masko.stalin.n.t/viz/PizzaSalesPerformanceDashboard_17836929985180/SalesPerformance?publish=yes

**Results:** 
* Segmented 32 Pizza SKUs based on **Product Performance matrix: Revenue Vs Volume** identified **11 hero products, 1 Volume Driver, 4 Premium Opportunities, and 16 underperforming products**.
  
  Recommendation:
    * **Hero products- Combo meals, inventory management, top menu placement(protect & grow core revenue).**
    * **Volume drivers-Pricing optimization, Upselling topping, Bundle with high margin products (increase AOV).**
    * **Premium Opportunities: Improve menu visibility, targeted promotions & offers (increase sales volume & maintain premium pricing).**
    * **Underperforming products: Test promotional campaigns, review pricing, recipe & menu placement, customer feedback (improve performance).**

* **Chicken pizzas** are the **top three pizzas, generating $127K (16% of total revenue) in sales**. The **Supreme category has the highest revenue**, while **revenue from Veggie pizzas is more evenly distributed**. Recommend focusing on **Classic pizzas for vegetarian customers and Chicken pizzas for non-vegetarian** customers through **targeted combos and offers**, while protecting the **Supreme category and optimizing Veggie pizzas through pricing and menu simplification.**

* **Large (L)** pizzas were the most preferred size with **12.74K orders**, followed by **Medium (11.16K)** and **Small (10.49K)**, while **XXL pizzas** accounted for only **0.03K orders**.Recommending **Optimize the inventory for L, M & S sizes, investigate on pricing for XXL & promotions, focus L & M for combos or family meals.**

* Identified **daily peak hours (12–2 PM, 4–8 PM) Thursday/Friday as highest-volume days** versus weekend lulls. Recommending **time-based staffing, dual promotion strategy(peak-hour upselling, off-peak BOGO(buy one get one)/loyalty programs)**.

* **Revenue fluctuated from +9.9% to -8.1% MoM** with no sustained growth trend, indicating promotion-driven rather than organic demand. Recommending a **consistent monthly promotional calendar**.

## Project: Olist Ecommerce Sales Analysis

**Project link:** https://github.com/NTMASKOSTALIN/Olist-Ecommerce-Dashboard

**Code:** 
SQL- https://github.com/NTMASKOSTALIN/Olist-Ecommerce-Dashboard/blob/main/2.%20ECommerce%20file.sql

Python- https://github.com/NTMASKOSTALIN/Olist-Ecommerce-Dashboard/blob/main/Olist%20EDA%20notebook.ipynb

**Goal:** Customer Retention & Revenue Optimization

**Description:** This project analyzes Olist’s Brazilian e-commerce marketplace data covering September 2016 to October 2018, comprising 95K+ orders across 9 relational tables, including Customers, Orders, Order Items, Order Payments, Order Reviews, Products, Sellers, Geolocation, and Product Category Names. The analysis follows an end-to-end data analytics workflow, including ETL, data profiling, data validation, join verification, master table creation, Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA), feature engineering, hypothesis testing, RFM analysis, cohort analysis, and Tableau dashboard development.

**Skills:** Data Profiling, Data Analysis, Data Manipulation, Statistical Analysis, Hypothesis Testing, Advanced Analytics, and Data Visualization.

**Technology:** SQL, Python & Tableau

**Dashboard:** https://public.tableau.com/app/profile/masko.stalin.n.t/viz/OlistEcommerceProject_17861174450860/CustomerRetentionChurn

**Results:** 
* **Customer Retention:** Identified a 40.99% customer retention rate, with 97% of customers making only one purchase and just 3% returning, highlighting a major repeat-purchase gap. Recommended loyalty programs, personalized campaigns, post-purchase engagement, and cross-selling to convert one-time buyers into repeat customers.

* **Customer Churn:** Identified a 59.01% churn rate, with 34.12% of customers classified as At-Risk, representing a significant opportunity for reactivation. Recommended targeted retargeting campaigns, personalized offers, and post-purchase email sequences to improve retention and repeat purchases.

* **RFM Segmentation:** Segmented customers into 40.81% Loyal, 34.12% At-Risk, and 16.72% High Value segments. The large At-Risk segment indicates a strong retention opportunity. Recommended personalized reactivation campaigns for At-Risk customers and loyalty incentives for High Value customers to maximize retention and revenue.

* **Revenue at Risk:** Identified $3.39M (22.03% of total $15.39M revenue) contributed by At-Risk customers, making them the largest immediate revenue-recovery opportunity. Their $106 Avg. Monetary value was substantially lower than High Value ($312) and Loyal ($184) customers. Recommended targeted reactivation campaigns, personalized offers, and loyalty incentives to move At-Risk customers into Loyal or High Value segments.

* **Delivery Performance:** Identified an average delivery delay of 10.62 days, with top delayed sellers reaching 15.16 days, contributing to poorer customer ratings. Recommended strengthening seller/carrier SLAs, prioritizing high-delay sellers, and improving delivery monitoring to reduce delays and improve customer experience.

* **Review Experience:** Identified 8,120 premature reviews averaging 2.77 stars, with premature reviews generating 43.18% 1-star ratings versus 6.72% for normal reviews. Recommended fixing premature review triggers and aligning review requests with actual delivery completion to improve the platform's 4.16 average review score at minimal operational cost.

* **Hypothesis Testing:** Customers with delayed deliveries showed a 59.11% churn rate vs. 57.77% for on-time deliveries, a 1.34 percentage-point difference. The Chi-Square test confirmed a statistically significant association (p = 0.00364, α = 0.05). However, the small practical difference indicates that delivery delays are associated with churn but are unlikely to be the primary driver. Recommended prioritizing customer retention, loyalty, personalized marketing, and repeat-purchase strategies alongside delivery improvements.

* **Revenue Concentration:** High Value and Loyal customers generated $11.57M (75% of total $15.39M revenue), demonstrating that a relatively smaller customer base contributes the majority of revenue. Recommended protecting high-value customers through loyalty programs while developing targeted strategies to move At-Risk customers into higher-value segments.

* **Overall Business Opportunity:** Analysis of 99,252 orders from September 2016 to October 2018 indicates that Olist's primary retention challenge is structural customer purchasing behavior rather than delivery experience alone. The strongest growth opportunity is to convert one-time and At-Risk customers into repeat buyers, while simultaneously improving delivery SLAs and review-trigger accuracy.
